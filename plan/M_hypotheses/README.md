# Plan: Track M — Hypotheses and Speculative Directions

[← Back to Plan Index](../README.md) | [ROADMAP Entry](../../ROADMAP/M_hypotheses.md) | [Execution Strategy](../EXECUTION_STRATEGY.md)

## Overview

Track M is a cross-cutting track that explores speculative and novel directions in theoretical physics. Unlike other tracks, it draws from **all** other tracks and does not have a strict sequential dependency — individual items can be explored as soon as the relevant foundations exist.

## Dependencies

**Upstream**: All tracks inform M (cross-cutting). Specific items depend on:
- [Track C](../C_quantum_mechanics/) — for M.1 (information-theoretic physics), M.4 (measurement problem)
- [Track D](../D_general_relativity/) — for M.2 (discrete spacetime), M.3 (problem of time)
- [Track E](../E_quantum_field_theory/) — for M.6 (hierarchy problem)
- [Track K](../K_quantum_gravity/) — for M.5 (cosmological constant), M.7 (multiverse)

**Downstream**: M informs [Track K](../K_quantum_gravity/) and [Track N](../N_experimental/) with new research directions.

## Implementation Strategy

Track M does not follow a linear implementation order. Instead:
1. Each item is explored when its prerequisites become available
2. Items are formalized as **conjectures** with precise statements
3. Connections between items are documented as they emerge
4. Items may be promoted to their own tracks if they gain sufficient structure
