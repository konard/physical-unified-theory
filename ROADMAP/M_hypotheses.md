## M.1 Novel Unification Proposals

### M.1.1 Information-Theoretic Foundations
- [ ] "It from Bit" (Wheeler): Physics from information
- [ ] Constructor theory (Deutsch and Marletto)
- [ ] Quantum Bayesianism (QBism) as a foundation
- [ ] Holographic principle as fundamental law
- [ ] Computational universe hypothesis

**Hypothesis M.1.1a**: *The fundamental laws of physics are information-theoretic constraints, and all physical quantities (space, time, energy, matter) are emergent from quantum information processing.*

**Hypothesis M.1.1b**: *Constructor theory — which reformulates physics in terms of which transformations are possible vs. impossible, rather than equations of motion — provides a more fundamental framework that naturally accommodates both quantum theory and general relativity.*

### M.1.2 Spacetime from Entanglement
- [ ] ER=EPR conjecture (Maldacena and Susskind)
- [ ] Spacetime geometry from entanglement entropy
- [ ] Tensor network / holography correspondence
- [ ] Quantum error correction and spacetime
- [ ] Entanglement wedge reconstruction

**Hypothesis M.1.2**: *Spacetime is woven from quantum entanglement — disconnecting entangled regions literally tears spacetime apart (ER=EPR), and the Einstein equations are the thermodynamic equations governing this entanglement structure.*

### M.1.3 Amplituhedron and Positive Geometry
- [ ] Scattering amplitudes beyond Feynman diagrams
- [ ] Associahedron for biadjoint scalar theory
- [ ] Positive geometry framework
- [ ] Emergent locality and unitarity
- [ ] Cosmological polytopes

**Hypothesis M.1.3**: *There exists a geometric object (generalizing the amplituhedron) whose structure encodes all scattering amplitudes in the complete theory of quantum gravity, making both spacetime and quantum mechanics emergent from geometry.*

### M.3.1 Approaches to the Problem
- [ ] Frozen formalism (timeless quantum gravity)
- [ ] Internal time (using matter fields as clocks)
- [ ] Thermal time hypothesis (Connes-Rovelli)
- [ ] Page-Wootters mechanism
- [ ] Emergent time from entanglement
- [ ] Relational approach

**Hypothesis M.3a**: *Time is not fundamental but emerges from quantum correlations — the Page-Wootters mechanism shows that a "timeless" universe can contain observers who experience time evolution.*

**Hypothesis M.3b**: *The thermal time hypothesis is correct: in quantum gravity, the flow of time is identified with the modular flow of the thermal state, and what we experience as time's passage is fundamentally thermodynamic.*

## M.4 The Measurement Problem and Gravity

- [ ] Penrose's objective reduction (OR)
- [ ] Diósi-Penrose model

**Hypothesis M.4b**: *The measurement problem in quantum mechanics is resolved by quantum gravity — the classical world emerges because macroscopic superpositions are gravitationally unstable, not because of any special role for observers.*

- [ ] Unimodular gravity
