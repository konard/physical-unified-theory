## G.2 Inflation

- [ ] Horizon and flatness problems
- [ ] Slow-roll inflation (single field)
- [ ] Inflationary perturbation theory
- [ ] Scalar and tensor power spectra
- [ ] Spectral index and tensor-to-scalar ratio
- [ ] Reheating after inflation
- [ ] Eternal inflation

**Hypothesis G.2a**: *A period of cosmic inflation occurred in the early universe, driven by a scalar field, and produced the primordial perturbations that seeded all structure in the universe.*

**Hypothesis G.2b**: *Eternal inflation implies a multiverse of pocket universes with different low-energy physics, and the specific parameters we observe are determined by anthropic selection rather than a unique theory.*
