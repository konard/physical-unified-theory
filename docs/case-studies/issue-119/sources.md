# Primary sources and reusable components

## Literature

| Source | Use in this case |
| --- | --- |
| [Chandrasekaran, arXiv:2611.34924v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34924v1) | The object under review. The versioned text fixes the conditions and conventions of every equation cited here. |
| [Choquet-Bruhat, Chruściel and Martín-García](https://arxiv.org/abs/1006.4467), [Chruściel](https://arxiv.org/abs/1209.1971), [Luk](https://arxiv.org/abs/1107.0898) | Characteristic constraints and local Einstein evolution. The light-cone and intersecting-null-hypersurface formulations have different data and regularity assumptions. Use them to state precisely the classical regime of a cut-and-match construction. |
| [Lehner, Myers, Poisson and Sorkin](https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.00207), [Chandrasekaran, Flanagan and Prabhu](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.11499), [Chandrasekaran and Flanagan](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.03871) | Independent derivations of null-boundary action terms, charges and phase space. Compare orientation, normalization and corner variables before transporting formulas. |
| [Ciambelli, Freidel and Leigh](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.03932), [Quantum Null Geometry and Gravity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.11132), [Ciambelli and Klinger](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07785) | Related null quantizations provide canonical and operator comparisons. Compare their clock and shear algebra on the same classical sector before trying to transfer estimates about allowed states. |
| [Buchholz and Fredenhagen](https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.06062) | Relates dynamical action-deformation relations to C*-algebras. A useful model for §4.1, with its own field content and hypotheses. |
| [Hollands and Wald](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0103074) | Renormalization of local Wick products and time-ordered products in curved spacetime. Relevant to short-distance subtraction and contact terms, subject to the tensor fields, gauge, state and boundary conditions used here. |
| [Colin-Ellerin et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.00828), [Fursaev, Patrushev and Solodukhin](https://arxiv.org/abs/1306.4000) | Lorentzian replica variational problem and conical curvature expansions. They give determinant and saddle checks, while identifying a saddle functional with an exact Hilbert-space replica limit remains necessary. |
| [Colafranceschi, Dong, Marolf and Wang](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.02189) | Algebras and Hilbert spaces reconstructed from Euclidean amplitudes under explicit axioms. Compare those axioms with Appendix E and verify the realization for the Lorentzian construction. |
| [Sen](https://arxiv.org/html/1205.0971#S1) | Independent one-loop coefficient for Schwarzschild. The mixed ensemble fixes mass and sums angular momenta. Converting from the logarithm of length to area gives the factor of one half. |
| [NIST DLMF §25.12](https://dlmf.nist.gov/25.12) | Polylogarithm normalization for the selected graviton channel. |

| Rocq Stdlib and [MathComp Analysis](https://github.com/math-comp/analysis) | Stdlib reals suffice for the independent proofs here. MathComp Analysis offers measure theory and normed analysis. A port should choose an operator and measure interface before trying to replay continuum limits. |
| [Einstein Toolkit](https://einsteintoolkit.org/) | Candidate for independent numerical evolution benchmarks. Start with a shear-free spherical solution, then compare a small shear perturbation and report discretization and constraint residuals. A simulation does not certify the nonlinear characteristic PDE or prove an operator limit. |

The dependency that dominates the remaining verification is the exact continuum model. An efficient construction would first prove the characteristic solution and the quantum estimates on matched regulated models of increasing resolution. It would then pass to the continuum only with uniform estimates sufficient for the domain, positivity and closed gluing claims. Numerics and one-loop agreement can detect errors during that process.
